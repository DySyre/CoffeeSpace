My.CoffeeSpace helps people discover nearby cafés through authentic, image-rich experiences shared by other coffee lovers, then capture their own visits with photos, ratings, stories, location, orders, and expenses. Users can follow creators, explore a map, track coffee spending, and optionally curate public shareable collections.
